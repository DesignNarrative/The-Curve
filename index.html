About The Curve

The Curve is located on Agashi Road, VIVA City, Bolinj, in Virar West, Vasai-Virar, Maharashtra – 401303. The project is just 7 minutes from Virar Railway Station and 3 minutes from the nearest rickshaw stand and bus stop, making it one of the best-connected new addresses in Virar West.
The Curve offers spacious 2 BHK and 3 BHK homes (3BHK Classic and 3BHK Premium) across 4 towers rising 33 storeys, with smart, functional layouts designed for maximum space utilisation.
The project features 40+ lifestyle amenities spread across two tiers — Ground Level (clubhouse, Aqua Hub covered pool, Adventure Zone for kids, Grand Ballroom, fitness zone, walking track, badminton court, social lounge) and Top Terrace Level (game lounge, wellness studio, kids’ play zone, work studio, outdoor dining, open-air theatre, and a 33rd-floor stargazing deck).
The Curve is developed by Abhinav Group, which has over 30 years of experience in real estate, 28+ completed projects, 10 million+ sq. ft. developed, and 5,000+ happy families across Mumbai.
Yes, the project is registered under MahaRERA with registration number PR1240002600667. Homebuyers can verify project details on the official MahaRERA portal (maharera.maharashtra.gov.in).
The Curve is close to Matrix Academy School (5 min), Muljibhai Mehta International School (8 min), Prakriti Hospital (4 min), Wellcare Multispeciality Hospital (11 min), D Mart (4 min), and The Capital Mall (11 min) — offering strong everyday connectivity for families.
Virar West is emerging as a well-connected residential micro-market with proximity to the railway station, upcoming infrastructure, and a mix of healthcare, retail, and educational institutions — making it an attractive option for both end-users and long-term investors seeking value appreciation.
The Curve is designed as a 33-storey architectural landmark with a distinctive curved facade and signature connected terraces — a design concept aimed at maximising light, air, and openness, which is uncommon in the Virar micro-market.
